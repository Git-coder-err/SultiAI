# SultiAI — Authors and Project Attribution

SultiAI is an academic capstone project originally conceived by **Kevin Albert A. Nisperos** and subsequently developed collaboratively by:

- **Kevin Albert A. Nisperos**
- **Genesis Diaz**
- **Jevan Adam Mulato**

## Project Origin

The initial concept and project direction for SultiAI were proposed by **Kevin Albert A. Nisperos**. The concept was subsequently refined, expanded, and developed collaboratively by the three team members into the SultiAI project.

## Collaborative Development

The development of SultiAI involves collaborative contributions from the three team members. Contributions may include project conceptualization, system design, software development, AI integration, documentation, research, testing, user evaluation, and other technical or creative work performed throughout the project.

## Project Identity

**SultiAI** is developed as an **AI Language Companion for Context-Aware Communication**.

The project is intended to help users better understand and participate in real-world communication by providing context-aware language assistance.

## Repository

This repository is maintained under the GitHub account of **Git-coder-err**. Team members may maintain independent branches or forks for development, experimentation, and collaboration.

A fork or independent branch represents a development copy of the project and does not, by itself, change the project's authorship or attribution.

Project authorship, intellectual property, and related rights are subject to applicable institutional policies, Philippine intellectual property laws, and any written agreements established among the project authors and the institution.
